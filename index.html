Mosaic

Assembled from emoji

Every emoji was drawn once at startup and its true average colour measured, allowing for how much of the tile it actually covers and what shows through behind it. Each tile then takes whichever emoji genuinely matches the colour under it. Settle stops them flickering between near-equal matches. Random abandons matching altogether, which is worth seeing once.
